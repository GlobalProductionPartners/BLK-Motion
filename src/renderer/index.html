Winch Library
Winch Models

BLK RISE — 75 cm/s lift, 1.2 kg max on cable, 3.3 m / 6 m variants, DMX512/RDM with power on the cable. Channels default to 75 cm/s or the model's rating, whichever is lower. DMX mode must match the fixture's LCD menu (5/6/9/10CH — the ES-L29 channel charts); it sets the patch footprint.

Fittings

The RISE addons — LED Sphere (150–350 mm) and LED Tube (1 m). Category shapes the 3D render (sphere point / vertical tube / batten bar); Size is the real physical dimension, drawn at true world scale, and tubes glow in the cue's colour.
